FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need to be technical?

Not at all. We handle everything — you just describe your problem in plain English. If you can explain how your business works over a phone call, we can automate it.

How long does it take to get set up?

Most simple automations are live within 3–5 business days after the audit call. More complex workflows typically take 1–2 weeks. We'll give you a clear timeline upfront.

What tools do you use?

We work with whatever you already have — your existing POS, email, forms, and spreadsheets. We don't force you to switch platforms. If you use Toast, Square, Google Workspace, or anything else, we build around it.

What if something breaks?

That's what the monthly retainer covers. We monitor your automations and fix issues fast — usually before you even notice. You're not on your own after launch.

What's the free audit, exactly?

It's a 30-minute call where we review your operations, identify your top 2–3 automation opportunities, and give you a clear action plan — whether you work with us or not. No pitch, no pressure.

I'm not in food & beverage — can you still help?

Probably. We started in F&B but the problems we solve — manual intake, data entry, ordering, follow-ups — exist in almost every service business. Reach out and we'll be honest about whether it's a good fit.
